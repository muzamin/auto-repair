Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Services

Clear, transparent answers regarding our automotive diagnostic procedures, parts warranty, and service booking.

Generic scan tools only read basic OBD-II emission codes. At VoltEdge, our certified technicians connect 4-channel PicoScope oscilloscopes directly to the vehicle's CAN-bus micro-circuits. We analyze voltage waveforms in real time, detecting microsecond drops, noisy sensor grounds, and communication bus collisions that handheld code scanners cannot see.

Modern vehicles equipped with Start-Stop, intelligent alternators, and smart Battery Management Systems (BMS) adjust their charging amperage as the battery ages. If a new battery is installed without reprogramming the ECU, the alternator will continue to overcharge the new battery, degrading its lifespan by up to 60% within months. We reset and calibrate the BMS to ensure 100% factory specifications.

All our premium AGM and lithium battery installations include a 36-month nationwide replacement warranty. Rebuilt alternators and starter motors carry a 24-month / 24,000-mile parts and labor guarantee. Custom wiring restorations and ECU adaptation services are backed by our 12-month zero-fault guarantee.

Yes, parasitic battery drain tracing is one of our flagship specialties. We utilize non-intrusive mA DC amp clamps, thermal imaging cameras, and millivolt drop fuse charting across sleeping control modules. We reliably isolate offending components (faulty alarm siren, stuck body relay, or wake-up line short) without randomly pulling fuses.

Yes. Our master technicians hold IMI Level 4 and NFPA 70E high-voltage safety certifications. We have dedicated insulated tooling, 1,000V rated safety gear, and calibrated Fluke Megohmmeters to safely perform de-energization, DC-DC converter diagnostics, and high-voltage insulation tests on 400V and 800V EV architectures.

Workshop diagnostic appointments can be scheduled online with same-day or next-morning availability. For roadside emergency jump-starts and mobile battery replacements, our mobile service units average an on-site arrival time of under 30 minutes within the metropolitan area.
