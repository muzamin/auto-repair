Our Mission: Eradicate Trial-and-Error With Scientific Telemetry

Every day, vehicle owners and commercial operators are told to replace multi-thousand-dollar modules because general mechanics cannot pinpoint intermittent electrical faults. 25-Year Chronological Journey

Milestones in Auto Electrical Engineering

From a single-bench oscilloscope repair shop to a nationally certified high-voltage automotive laboratory.

01
2001 • Genesis

The Dedicated Oscilloscope Lab

Founded with dual analog cathode-ray oscilloscopes, specialized in carbureted ignition timing, diode pack replacements, and heavy starter motor rebuilding.

02
2008 • Multiplex Era

CAN-Bus Protocol Integration

Introduced high-speed serial bus data loggers, decoding the very first generation of CAN and LIN networks on luxury European and Japanese automobiles.

03
2015 • Heavy-Duty Expansion

High-Output Alternator Facility

Commissioned our dedicated 250A stator winding bench and variable-speed dyno load tester to serve emergency response ambulances and refrigerated haulers.

04
2020 • Clean-Room Launch

800V EV Battery Isolation Chamber

Built an ESD-safe clean chamber for hybrid and EV battery module conductance balancing, internal resistance tracing, and ground fault localization.
