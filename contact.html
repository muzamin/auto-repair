Helpful information on what to expect when booking an appointment, bringing your vehicle, or requesting an estimate.

We welcome drive-in walk-ins for quick 15-minute battery health checks and replacements during workshop hours. For complex oscilloscope diagnostic traces, CAN-Bus troubleshooting, or parasitic battery drain investigations, we recommend booking an appointment online to ensure an allocated diagnostic lift and specialist technician.

Please bring both keys/fobs if your issue relates to keyless entry or immobilizer faults. Note down when the symptoms occur (e.g. cold start vs warm engine, rainy days, driving over bumps). If you had recent repairs or another shop scanned your vehicle, bringing previous printouts is very helpful!
